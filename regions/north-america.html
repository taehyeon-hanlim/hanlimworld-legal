  • Directly from you (account registration, video upload)
  • Automatically during service use (app analytics, logs)
  • From Google OAuth (if you sign in with Google)

1.3 Business/Commercial Purposes

  • Providing the requested AI analysis service
  • Security, debugging, short-term transient use
  • Quality/safety maintenance and improvement

1.4 Sale & Sharing

Hanlimworld does not sell personal information for monetary or other valuable consideration, and does not share personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising. The "Do Not Sell or Share My Personal Information" link is therefore not applicable; however, you may still request deletion or access at any time.

1.5 California Consumer Rights

  • Right to Know: categories, sources, purposes, third parties.
  • Right to Access: specific pieces of personal information.
  • Right to Correct: inaccurate personal information.
  • Right to Delete: personal information we collect.
  • Right to Limit Use of Sensitive PI: not applicable (we don't collect sensitive PI per CPRA definition).
  • Right to Non-Discrimination: exercising rights will not result in service denial, price change, or quality reduction.
  • Shine the Light (Civil Code §1798.83): we do not share PI with third parties for their direct marketing purposes.

Submit requests to privacy@hanlimworld.com. We will verify your identity via the email associated with your account. Responses within 45 days (extendable to 90 days with notice). Authorized agents must provide written permission and identity verification.

2. Other US State Laws

Users in Virginia (VCDPA), Colorado (CPA), Connecticut (CTDPA), Utah (UCPA), Texas (TDPSA), Oregon (OCPA), Montana (MCDPA), and other states with similar privacy laws are granted substantially equivalent rights (access, correction, deletion, portability, opt-out of targeted advertising/profiling). 3. Children's Online Privacy (COPPA, 15 U.S.C. §6501–6506)

The Service is not directed to children under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If we learn we have collected such information, we will delete it promptly. Parents/guardians may contact privacy@hanlimworld.com to request deletion.

4. Canada — PIPEDA

For users in Canada, Hanlimworld complies with the Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA) and, where applicable, Quebec Law 25, Alberta PIPA, and British Columbia PIPA.

4.1 Fair Information Principles

We adhere to the ten PIPEDA principles: Accountability, Identifying Purposes, Consent, Limiting Collection, Limiting Use/Disclosure/Retention, Accuracy, Safeguards, Openness, Individual Access, Challenging Compliance.

4.2 Privacy Officer

Privacy Officer: Hanlimworld Privacy Team · privacy@hanlimworld.com

4.3 Complaints

Canadian users may file complaints with the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada: priv.gc.ca.

4.4 Quebec Law 25

Quebec residents have additional rights including data portability, transparency regarding automated decision-making, and a dedicated Privacy Officer contact. Hanlimworld does not engage in automated decisions that produce legal or similar significant effects; AI scores are advisory only.
